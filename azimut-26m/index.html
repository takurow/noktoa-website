Interior

The model's exterior is by Alberto Mancini and, according to Azimut, the interior by Achille Salvagni; individual boats may vary. Pale timber, cobalt accents and full-height glazing on both sides of the main deck.

On board

Layout

Owner's suite on the main deck. Two VIP and two guest cabins on the lower deck. Captain and crew forward, with their own stairs. Choose a deck, then a point on the drawing, to see that space on board.

Standard general arrangement; individual boats may vary. Drawing: Azimut standard GA. All photographs are of this boat.

Flybridge and aft deck

The flybridge is one level, about 50 m², with the helm, sunpads and dining under the hardtop. Aft, the Deck2Deck™ terrace extends the transom into outdoor dining for eight.

  • Flybridge ≈ 50 m², single level
  • Deck2Deck™ transom terrace, dining for eight
  • Carbon-fibre superstructure, about 30% lighter
  • Bow lounge with sunpads and table

Figures per Azimut technical specification for the model.

Engines and propulsion

Two MAN V12s of 1,650 hp each drive ZF POD 4600 units. With the carbon-fibre superstructure, Azimut quotes about 20% lower fuel consumption in the usual cruising band. Per the model specification: 28 knots maximum, 24 knots cruising, 369 nautical miles at cruise.
